    We have a policy to deliver and no plan for it

    Usually Strategy

    A plan your teams can act on.

    A policy says what should change. A plan says who does what first. We work with your staff and the people the policy is for to turn the ambition into steps, and we test those steps before anyone commits real money.

    How we would usually start

    A short discovery, usually four to eight weeks: research with the people involved and a clear plan you can take to your board.

    Our services don’t join up

    Usually Strategy

    One service, seen from the user’s side.

    People do not see your departments. They see one organisation and one journey, with the gaps in it. We map that journey as they experience it, find where it breaks, and decide with you where to start.

    How we would usually start

    A service map built with staff from each part of the journey, over two or three workshops.

    We don’t really know the people we serve

    Usually Labs

    Research with the public, not about them.

    Interviews, diaries and workshops that show how people really live with a service or an idea, in their own words. Then we bring what we heard back to your team in a form you can use.

    How we would usually start

    A short round of research with the people the service is for, written up so your team can act on it.

    We’re launching something new

    Usually Brand

    A name, a look and a campaign people understand.

    We start with what people already know and do, and build the identity and the campaign from there. Circular.ie went from nothing to a national campaign on one idea: show people they are already doing it.

    How we would usually start

    A naming and positioning sprint of about four weeks, tested with the public before anything is designed.

    Our website doesn’t work for everyone

    Usually Digital

    A site built for everyone who uses it.

    We build on open tools, to WCAG 2.2 AA, and test with real users and assistive technology. Pages are light, so they load on a slow connection, and we stay on after launch to keep improving them.

    How we would usually start

    An accessibility and content review of what you have now, with a list of fixes in order.

    Our brand no longer fits who we are

    Usually Brand

    An identity that says what you do now.

    Organisations change and their brands lag behind. We work out what you stand for today, with your staff and your audiences, and give you an identity and guidelines your own team can use well.

    How we would usually start

    A brand review with your team and a few of your audiences, in about three weeks.

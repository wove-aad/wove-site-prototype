    Accessibility

    We want everyone to be able to use this website.

    Here’s how we try, and what still needs work.

    StandardWCAG 2.2 AA
    Contacthello@wove.group

    How we built it

    We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Every page works with a keyboard alone, has a skip link, and uses headings, labels and landmarks that screen readers can follow.

    Movement on the site respects your device’s reduced motion setting. The Aa button at the top of every page makes the text larger, and the setting is saved on your device.The Aa button on every page opens a short list of settings, so you can change the text size, movement, pictures, cursor, contrast, typeface and links. They are saved on your device.

    What isn’t right yet

    Some older project photos need better descriptions, and some downloadable documents are not yet accessible. We’re fixing these as we go.

    Tell us about a problem

    If something on this site doesn’t work for you, email hello@wove.group. We’ll reply within five working days and tell you what we’re doing about it.

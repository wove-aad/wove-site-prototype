Our work/The Abbey Theatre

We worked with The Abbey Theatre, Ireland’s National Theatre, to rethink how a 120-year-old institution interacts with a changing public in a digital world.

ClientThe Abbey Theatre, Ireland’s National Theatre
ThemesArts and culturePublic services
What we didCo-design and collaboration, research and insights, user journeys
Talk toScott Burnett
Director, Insight and Strategy
120

years old. A national body with a founding mission, meeting a changing public.

5

groups joined up behind one experience: co-directors, producers, marketing, operations and front of house.

The way here

The Abbey Theatre is Ireland’s National Theatre, with a founding mission to help the changing nation understand where we come from, where we are, and where we’re going.

Digital change and audience shifts after Covid were driving its latest evolution. As part of a wider programme of strategic co-design, we worked with the Abbey to re-imagine how it interacts with the groups, communities and audiences it serves.

How the work went
  1. Research and mappingThe organisation and its audiences
  2. Drivers and frameworksTo align thinking and action
  3. Brand updateWith aad, for a digital landscape
  4. The whole journeyUsed as a canvas for engagement

The way through

Research and mapping built a full picture of how the organisation works and how audience expectations were shifting: how to balance history and custodianship with agility and ambition.

We defined a set of drivers and frameworks to line up thinking and action, connecting the work of a wide range of people with one joined-up experience for a much wider set of audiences.

The way forward

The work increased creativity and collaboration across teams, giving them room to be more ambitious with how the Abbey meets the world.

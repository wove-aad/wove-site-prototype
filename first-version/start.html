What we do/Where to start

Not sure where to start? Pick the situation closest to yours, and we’ll say how we would begin.

What are you trying to change?

A plan your teams can act on.

A policy says what should change. A plan says who does what first. We work with your staff and the people the policy is for to turn the ambition into steps, and we test those steps before anyone commits real money.

How we would usually start

A short discovery, usually four to eight weeks: research with the people involved and a clear plan you can take to your board.

Mostly Strategy, with other teams as the work needs them.

Talk to Karl about it

Karl Toomey, Labs Director

Talk to Karl More about Karl

A name, a look and a campaign people understand.

We start with what people already know and do, and build the identity and the campaign from there. Circular.ie went from nothing to a national campaign on one idea: show people they are already doing it.

How we would usually start

A naming and positioning sprint of about four weeks, tested with the public before anything is designed.

Mostly Brand, with other teams as the work needs them.

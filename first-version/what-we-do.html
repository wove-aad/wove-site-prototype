What we do

You have a policy to deliver, a service to fix or a change to land with staff and the public. We help you do it with the people it affects.

Why Wove?

Why Wove? Because the work is woven. Research, strategy, brand and digital, made together, and made with the people who will use it.

Our four teams work as one. Most projects use two or three of them. Some, like Circular.ie, use all four.

Our four teams

Labs

Try an idea before you commit to it.

Karl ToomeyLabs Director
Talk to Karl

Research and development for a more human future.

  • Research with the publicInterviews, diaries and workshops that show how people really live with a service or an idea.
  • Co-design workshopsSessions where staff and the public shape the work together, in person or online.
  • ProvotypesQuick, rough versions of an idea, made to start a conversation rather than to be finished.
  • Testing smallTrying an idea with a few people before anyone commits money or time to it.
  • FuturesLooking ten years ahead to test today’s plans, in our What if…? series and with clients.

How a project usually runs

Not every project needs every step. Most go round the middle three more than once.

  1. ListenTo the people the work is for, and the staff who will run it.
  2. MapThe whole picture, so everyone sees the same thing.
  3. TryIdeas small, before anyone commits real money.
  4. MakeThe service, the brand or the plan that worked.
  5. Look after itAfter launch, improving it as people use it.

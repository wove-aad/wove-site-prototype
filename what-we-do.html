    You have a policy to deliver, a service to fix or a change to land with staff and the public. We help you do it with the people it affects.

    Four teams

    Labs

    Try an idea before you commit to it.

    Research and development for a more human future.

    Strategy

    Turn a policy into a plan your teams can act on.

    Helping organisations plan, prioritise and change in lasting ways.

    Brand

    Explain the change so the public and your staff get it.

    Identities, content and campaigns that culturally connect.

    Digital

    Build the service, and keep it working after launch.

    Platforms and innovation that help you deliver and scale impact.

    Woven together on one project

    Circular.ie

    Circular.ie is a ten-year learning project on the circular economy. All four teams have worked on it, and the work is still growing.

    LabsCitizen workshops across Ireland, to learn what circular living means to people.StrategyFour frameworks that turn national policy into steps people can take.BrandA name, a circle and an ownable yellow, then a national campaign.DigitalOne platform for individuals, communities and researchers, built to last ten years.

    How a project usually runs

    ListenTo the people the work is for, and the staff who will run it.
    MapThe whole picture, so everyone sees the same thing.
    TryIdeas small, before anyone commits real money.
    MakeThe service, the brand or the plan that worked.
    Look after itAfter launch, improving it as people use it.

    Ways to work with us

    A short discovery

    Research with the people involved and a clear plan you can take to your board or management team.

    Usually four to eight weeks

    A programme

    Strategy, brand and digital built up over time, the way our Circular.ie work grew.

    Six months to a few years

    Support after launch

    We look after a service you already have and keep improving it, month by month.

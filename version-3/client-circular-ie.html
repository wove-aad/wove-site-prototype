    Work / Circular.ie

    Circular.ie logoCircular.ie

    Working with The Rediscovery Centre on Circular.ie since 2025, across all four of our teams.

    Client since: 2025Projects: 5Teams: All fourTheme: Public services

    We’ll email you when we add something to Circular.ie. Nothing else, and you can stop at any time.

    You’re following Circular.ie. We’ll write to when something is added.

    Ireland’s shift to a circular economy has plenty of policy activity, but little to help ordinary people take part. With the Rediscovery Centre, we started with the foundations: four award-winning strategic frameworks that turn national ambition into everyday action. Everything since, from the brand and platform to the national campaign, is built on them.

    Launch of Circular Lab at The Rediscovery Centre

    Studio update · Circular.ie

    Circular Lab opened at The Rediscovery Centre.

    Grace Mc Ginn, Digital Director

    17 Sep 2026

    ShareEmail
    KindStudio update
    ThemePublic services
    TopicCircular economy
    WithCircular.ie
    Written byGrace Mc Ginn

    Circular Lab opened at The Rediscovery Centre in Ballymun this week: a place to try circular ideas out in public, and the first physical home for the Circular.ie platform.

    Grace was there for the launch. More photos, and a proper write-up, to follow once the lab has had a few weeks of visitors.

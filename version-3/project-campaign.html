Project · Circular.ie · Brand

How do you launch a national campaign for something people have never heard of?

For Circular.ie, the answer was to show people they’re already doing it.

Johnny Kelly · Brand and Strategy · 2025 · 4 minute read

Client
Circular.ie
Impact areas
Circular economy, Public services
Teams
Brand, Strategy
Part of
Circular.ie, a ten-year programme
Talk to
Johnny Kelly

The challenge

The research told us that leading with environmental messaging doesn’t motivate action.

After months of co-creation workshops across Ireland, we had a clear brand strategy (“Uncomplicated Progress”) and a platform ready to go. But we needed a campaign that would cut through, reach diverse audiences, and establish foundational data for a 10-year learning project.

Because at its heart, Circular.ie is a research initiative. Everything is designed for testing, learning, and iterating what works.

The idea

Show people they’re already doing it.

The TV ads followed a deliberate progression. We started with single, highly relatable circular actions, then moved toward group actions that might be less familiar but still accessible.

A launch across channels

Working with our production partners TinyArk, we delivered the campaign across TV and on demand, radio, digital advertising with A/B testing built in, and social content using real photography.

The impact

Testing is still underway, but early indicators show the campaign is succeeding in making the circular economy feel accessible and relevant to a mass audience, while establishing the data foundation needed for long-term learning.

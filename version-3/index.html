Woven together on one project

Circular.ie

Circular.ie is a ten-year learning project on the circular economy. All four teams have worked on it, and the work is still growing.

  • LabsCitizen workshops across Ireland, to learn what circular living means to people.
  • StrategyFour frameworks that turn national policy into steps people can take.
  • BrandA name, a circle and an ownable yellow, then a national campaign.
  • DigitalOne platform for individuals, communities and researchers, built to last ten years.

    About

    A strategic design studio in Dublin. We help public bodies, cultural organisations and purpose-led businesses change the services they run, and we stay with them for years rather than months.

    StudioBrigid House, 38 Clarendon Street, Dublin 2
    PeopleTwelve, in four teams
    TeamsLabs, Strategy, Brand, Digital
    CertifiedB Corp

    Where we come from

    Wove Group is two Dublin studios brought together. Wove started in strategic design and research with the public. aad started in brand and digital design for cultural organisations and purpose-led businesses.

    Our clients kept coming to one studio and then needing the other. What we learned in research would be handed on before it reached a brand, a service or a website. Working as one studio means the same people carry it the whole way.

    That is why we work the way we do now: in longer relationships, with more than one of our teams on the same problem.

    How we work

    We start by listening, to the people a change affects and to the staff who will run it. They come into the work with us, rather than hearing about it at the end.

    We try ideas small before anyone commits real money, and we keep what we make light: plain words, accessible by default, and websites that use less energy.

    And we tend to stay. Most of our clients come back for a second project, and the best of what we do comes from what the first one taught us.

    What we believe

    Start with peopleThe people a change affects help shape it, from the first workshop.
    Stay for the long runWe would rather build with a client over years than hand over and leave.
    Work across teamsResearch, strategy, brand and digital on the same problem, in the same room.
    Try it small firstTest an idea cheaply before anyone commits real money or time.
    Keep it lightPlain words, accessible by default, and websites that use less energy.
    Leave teams strongerTools and skills stay with the client when we step back.
